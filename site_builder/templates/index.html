Do developer-adoption signals lead revenue?

This site tracks how fast developers and companies adopt AI and data-infrastructure tools, using free public data: package downloads, Ask HN: Who is hiring? job posts and GitHub. It then tests whether those signals lead reported revenue at six public data-infrastructure companies (SNOW, DDOG, MDB, CFLT, ESTC, NET).
